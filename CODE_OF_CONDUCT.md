# Community Conduct

SAIPEN HQ welcomes technical disagreement. It does not require performative
agreement.

Keep participation focused on the work:

- criticize implementations, claims, and decisions rather than attacking people;
- provide evidence when making technical claims that others need to act on;
- do not harass, threaten, dox, impersonate, or deliberately expose private data;
- do not flood issues or discussions with repetitive, automated, or off-topic content;
- respect repository-specific contribution and security boundaries;
- accept that maintainers may close discussions that are abusive, unsafe, spammy,
  or no longer useful to the project.

Good-faith mistakes can be corrected. Repeated boundary violations may result in
content removal, moderation, or loss of participation access.
